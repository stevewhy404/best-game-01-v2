How to Play

  • Steering & Sails: Use A/D (or Left/Right) to steer. Use W/S (or Up/Down) to set sail speed. Watch the wind compass! Sailing with the wind is fast; against it is slow.
  • Ports & Trading: Sail close to any of the 5 island ports and press E to dock. Buy low, sell high, pick up delivery contracts, repair hull, and buy ship upgrades.
  • Combat: Pirates roam the seas. Press Space or click to fire broadside cannons toward your mouse cursor. Defeat them for gold and loot!
  • Hazards: Avoid rocky shores and moving storm cells.
  • Victory: Reach 10,000 Gold to achieve Legendary Captain status!
